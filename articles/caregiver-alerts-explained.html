How Caregiver Alerts Work: Keeping Family in the Loop Without Constant Phone Calls

By the SentraX Forte Limited editorial team2 min readUpdated 6 November 2026

When someone you love lives with high blood pressure or diabetes, worry often turns into repeated phone calls: Did you take your tablet? What was your reading? Are you feeling alright? These check-ins come from care, but they can feel tiring for both sides. Alerts that reach the right family member at the right moment can replace many of those calls and still make sure nothing important is missed.

In Sentra-X, a person can save family members as caregivers. When a medicine is overdue, or a blood pressure reading is in a risky range, the app can send alerts to those caregivers by SMS and email, so they do not need to keep asking. The person can also use the emergency button, which sends a message with their location to all saved caregivers by SMS and email and prepares a WhatsApp message for the primary caregiver. The emergency alert supports a phone call to emergency services and does not replace one.

To make alerts useful, agree on a plan in advance. Decide who the primary caregiver is, make sure phone numbers and emails are correct, and talk about what each person should do when an alert arrives, for example call first, then visit or contact a neighbour if there is no answer. Review the list from time to time, especially if someone changes number. A good alert system gives families peace of mind while respecting the independence of the person being cared for.

Key takeaways

  • Alerts can replace many routine check-in calls.
  • Keep caregiver phone numbers and emails up to date.
  • Agree in advance who responds first and what they do.

Quick FAQ

Does an app alert replace calling emergency services?

No. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number straight away. Alerts are a fast way to let family know, not a substitute for professional help.
