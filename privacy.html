✚ Sentra-X

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Sentra-X ("we," "the app") helps you track vitals, manage medication, and stay connected with a caregiver. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and who it's shared with.

Sentra-X is currently in testing. This policy will be reviewed and formalized before any wider public release.

What We Collect

When you use Sentra-X, we collect information you provide directly:

CategoryExamples
AccountEmail address, password (handled securely by Firebase Authentication — we never see your raw password)
ProfileYour first name, a general health condition you choose to share
VitalsBlood pressure, heart rate, weight readings you log, and timestamps
MedicationsMedication names, schedules, and whether doses were marked taken
Health PassportOptional fields you choose to fill in: blood group, genotype, allergies, conditions, medical history, vaccinations, height, physician, insurance info, emergency contact
Caregiver infoA caregiver's name, phone number, and email, if you choose to add one
AI AssistantMessages you send to the in-app health assistant

You choose what to enter. Nothing listed above is required to use the core app beyond an account.

How We Use It

  • To show you your own data back (vitals history, medication schedule, adherence stats)
  • To send you medication reminders, on your device
  • To alert a caregiver you've added — by SMS, email, or a WhatsApp message you send yourself — if a dose is significantly overdue or a reading is high-risk
  • To let a linked caregiver view (never edit) your data through their own separate account, only if you generate and share an invite code with them
  • To respond to questions you ask the AI health assistant

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who We Share It With

We use a small number of external services to run the app. Each only receives the specific data needed to do its job:

ServiceWhat it's forWhat it receives
Google FirebaseAccount login and data storageYour account data and everything you log in the app
Anthropic (via a Cloudflare Worker)Powers the AI health assistantOnly the messages you send to the assistant — not your vitals, medications, or account info
EmailJSSends caregiver alert emailsYour caregiver's email address and the alert message
TermiiSends caregiver alert SMSYour caregiver's phone number and the alert message
WhatsAppLets you manually send an alert or invite linkOnly what's in the message you choose to send — this opens WhatsApp on your own device, we don't transmit anything to WhatsApp ourselves

Data Security

Your data is protected by access rules that only allow your own account (and a caregiver you've explicitly linked) to read it. No one else can access your health data through the app.

Your Choices

  • Any Health Passport field is optional and can be left blank or cleared at any time
  • You can stop sharing with a caregiver by not generating new invite codes; a linked caregiver's access can currently be removed by contacting us (see below)
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us

Children's Privacy

Sentra-X is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is used, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
