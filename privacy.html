✚ Sentra-X

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (version 2026-11-16)

Sentra-X Forte Limited ("we", "Sentra-X") runs the Sentra-X app and website. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

In short: you decide who sees your health data. You can download it, delete it, and withdraw consent at any time (Family tab, under Your data and privacy, or delete-account page). We never sell your data.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it in readable form).
  • Profile details you choose to add: name, condition, sex, an optional profile photo, pregnancy dates if you use that tracker, and medical passport details such as blood group, genotype, allergies and medications.
  • Health data you enter or measure: blood pressure, heart rate, weight, symptoms, notes, medication schedules and whether you took them, water, sleep, mood and activity logs, optional pregnancy tracking, and photos you attach to medication records.
  • Caregiver and emergency contact details: the names, phone numbers and email addresses of people you add. Please ask them first.
  • Location: your phone's location, only when you press Emergency SOS (and in the background while the app is open to decide which emergency number to show). It is not tracked continuously.
  • Camera and microphone: the camera is used for the heart-rate reading, document scanning and the dance game. The heart-rate and dance analysis happens on your phone and the video is not stored or sent. The microphone is used only for voice input.
  • Technical data: device and browser type, error reports, and the notification token needed to send reminders to your phone.
  • Purchases: if you buy in the marketplace, order details and the payment reference. Card details go directly to Paystack and are not seen by us.

4. AI assistant, document scanning and your caregivers

  • AI assistant: your questions are sent to an AI model provider through our Cloudflare service to write a reply. We do not send your readings, medicines or condition unless you switch on "include my health details" when you give AI consent. You can change this or withdraw consent in the Family tab under Your data and privacy, then AI privacy settings. AI answers are general information, not medical advice.
  • Document scanning: the photo you pick is sent to an AI service to read the text, and only after you agree. The provider may process it outside Nigeria. Avoid photos with faces, ID numbers or other people.
  • Caregivers: a caregiver you invite can read the health information in your account. They cannot change it. You can see your linked caregivers and remove their access at any time in the Family tab.
  • Voice input: uses your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome, audio may be sent to Google to turn it into text. Sentra-X does not record or keep your voice.

5. Emergency SOS and the Bayelsa State emergency line

When you press Emergency SOS and confirm, we send your name, live location and a message to the caregivers you have saved. If your phone's location shows that you are in Bayelsa State, or we cannot tell where you are, we also send your name, live location and your caregiver's contact number by SMS and email to the Bayelsa State emergency line so responders can reach you. Outside Bayelsa we do not send to that line, and the app shows the number that fits your area instead. SOS messages cannot be recalled once sent.

6. Where your data goes (international transfers)

Our cloud database, hosting and several providers above (including Google, Cloudflare, Sentry, EmailJS and the AI provider) process data outside Nigeria. We rely on their contractual protections and choose providers that publish data-protection commitments. If you want details about where a particular type of data is processed, contact us at the address below.

8. How we protect it

  • Data moves over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored with Google's encryption at rest.
  • Database rules mean only you can read and write your record. A linked caregiver can read it but not change it.
  • The optional quick-unlock PIN encrypts your saved login on your phone.
  • Data saved on your phone is protected by your phone's own lock. The app does not separately encrypt it, so please use a screen lock, and use "Log out and erase data on this phone" on shared devices.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours, and we will tell you without undue delay.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or give you a copy to move elsewhere. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. You also have the right not to be subject to decisions made only by automated means that significantly affect you; our risk checks and AI answers are guidance, not decisions about you.

  • Download your data: Family tab, "Download my data".
  • Delete your account and data: Family tab, "Delete my account", or see how to request deletion.
  • Withdraw AI consent: Family tab, "AI privacy settings".
  • Anything else: contact us below. We reply within 30 days.

You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

10. Children

Sentra-X is for adults. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must create and manage the account and give consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children without that consent. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Cookies and ads

The Health Library and marketplace show Google AdSense ads, labelled "Ad". Ads are never shown on your readings, medications, history or caregiver screens. Until you choose "Allow personalised", ads are not personalised. You can also manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app and update the date above.
