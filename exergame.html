Before you start

Sentra Quest is gentle, guided movement. A few quick safety points:

If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, are pregnant, or haven't exercised in a long time, check with your doctor first. Start slowly and use Seated mode if standing is hard.

Stop and rest if you feel dizzy, short of breath, have chest pain, or any pain. Call your emergency number if chest pain or breathlessness doesn't ease.
