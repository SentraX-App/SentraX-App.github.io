Delete your Sentra-X account

Last updated: 16 November 2026

You can delete your Sentra-X account and the personal data we hold in it at any time.

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sentra-X and log in.
  2. Go to the Family tab and scroll to Your data and privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account, readings, medicines, photos and caregiver invites are deleted straight away, and your caregivers lose access.

Option 2: ask us to do it

If you cannot log in, email us from the address you registered with. Use the subject "Delete my account" and tell us the email address of the account.

sentraxforteltd@gmail.com. We verify it is you, then delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: your account, health records, profile, medication photos, invites, notification token and caregiver links.
  • Not in our control: SMS and email alerts already sent to your caregivers, and short-term records kept by our service providers (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Kept if the law requires: payment records for purchases.
  • On your phone: clearing the app or using "Log out and erase data on this phone" removes anything saved locally.

Want a copy first? In the app, Family tab, under Your data and privacy, tap Download my data.
