Editorial Policy

The Sentra-X Health Library exists to give people clear, general health information in plain language. This page explains how we work.

How articles are written

Articles are written by the Sentra-X Forte Limited editorial team for general education. We aim to be accurate, practical and easy to read, and to explain when someone should seek care rather than try to manage a problem alone.

Sources

We draw on guidance from established reference organisations such as the World Health Organization, the American Heart Association and the NHS (UK). Each article lists the reference organisations for further reading.

What our articles are not

Our articles are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and they are not a substitute for a qualified health professional. We do not tell readers to start, stop or change a medicine. Always speak to a doctor, nurse or pharmacist about your own situation, and seek emergency care immediately if you think someone is in danger.

Updates and corrections

Each article shows the date it was last updated. We revise articles when guidance changes or when readers point out mistakes. To report a problem, use our contact page.

Advertising

Sentra-X shows Google AdSense advertisements only in the Health Library and the marketplace. Ads are labelled "Ad", and they never appear on patient screens such as readings, medications, history or the caregiver dashboard. Advertisers do not influence what we write.
