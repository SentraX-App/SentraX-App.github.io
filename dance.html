Before you dance

Sentra Flow Dance is gentle, rhythmic movement to get your blood flowing.

Space and support: clear the area around you and keep a sturdy chair or wall nearby. Choose Seated if balance is a worry.

Stop if you feel dizzy, short of breath, or have chest pain or any pain. If you have a heart condition or high blood pressure, check with your doctor first.

Your privacy: the camera is analysed only on your phone, in real time. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded. You can also play without the camera.

Why rhythmic movement?

Rhythmic movement of the legs and arms makes your muscles squeeze blood back toward the heart, and regular activity supports healthy blood pressure. Dance-based exercise has also been studied for balance and fitness in older adults. This is a fun way to move, not a medical treatment.
