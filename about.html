About Sentra-X

Sentra-X is a family health protection app built by Sentra-X Forte Limited, a company registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria (RC 9718956) and based in Bayelsa State.

What the app does

Sentra-X helps people living with long-term conditions such as hypertension and diabetes keep track of their health and stay on schedule with their medication. You can log blood pressure readings, set medication reminders, and see your own history over time.

The app also lets a trusted family member or friend act as a caregiver. If a medication is missed, the patient is reminded first, and the caregiver is alerted only if it is still not taken. An Emergency SOS button can send an alert to saved contacts by SMS, email and WhatsApp.

Who it is for

Sentra-X is for patients, and for the relatives who worry about them, especially when family members live in different places and cannot check in every day. It is meant to support the care people already receive from doctors, nurses and pharmacists, never to replace it.
